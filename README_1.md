# Doodley Music

**A doodle-style music player for Windows, with a pixel companion who dances to your music.**

Doodle lives in a cosy little pixel room. They dance on the rug when music plays, study at the desk when it's quiet,
and sleep in bed at night. Sometimes a cat drops by.

## Download

👉 **[Download the latest version](https://github.com/Doodiez/doodley-music/releases/latest)**: get `Doodley-Music-Setup-x.y.z.exe` and run it.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed. Click **More info → Run anyway**.

## Features

- 🎵 **Add music your way:** drop in your MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG or M4A files, or paste a link (YouTube, SoundCloud,
  Bandcamp and more) to save it as an MP3 with its title, artist and cover art
- 💿 **A real record to scratch:** drag the vinyl to scratch, hold it to rewind
- 🕺 **Doodle, your companion:** dances to every genre, talks in pixel speech bubbles, picks a favourite song and
  asks for it when the music stops. Dress Doodle up in the closet with notes you earn
- 🔊 **Listen to PC:** Doodle dances to anything your PC plays, from Spotify to games
- 🪩 **Disco party:** lively songs drop a disco ball into the room and the lights dance to the beat
- 🎨 **Three styles:** Doodle (hand-drawn), Studio Glass and Record Shop, each in light and dark
- 🎡 **Song roulette:** can't choose? Spin the wheel
- 🎮 **Discord status:** show what you're listening to on your Discord profile
- 📃 Playlists, search, renaming, crossfade, a mini player and media keys

## Privacy

Doodley Music doesn't collect anything about you or your music. It only goes online to check for updates and to
download the links you give it.
